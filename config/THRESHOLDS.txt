Release gate thresholds were configured before the candidate experiment.

Recall@5: at most 0.02 absolute regression. With 20 answerable questions, a
single completely lost question costs 0.05, so the gate rejects that loss.
Faithfulness proxy: at least 0.80 among answered cases. Baseline measured 0.8125.
This is a task-specific fact-and-cited-context check, not complete entailment.
Citation membership: at least 0.95; baseline measured 1.0.
Unanswerable abstention: 1.0, and no drop from baseline (baseline 1.0).
Answerable success: at least 0.50, to stop an always-abstaining candidate from
passing safety-oriented metrics. Baseline measured 0.60.
Latency p95: at most 180 seconds on this local CPU setup; baseline measured
80.408 seconds. The budget leaves room for retries but rejects multi-minute
tail latency. It is an educational local-demo budget, not a production SLA.
HTTP/evaluation errors: zero.

Coverage uses cosine >= 0.85 and requires >= 0.85 of live queries covered.
This cutoff is stricter than broad topical resemblance. With the fixed 40-query
fixture it covered all 28 in-domain queries initially and none of the 12 new-topic
queries. After adding six explicit new-topic tests it covered 37/40. This is
resubstitution on a designed log, not evidence of generalization to held-out users.
